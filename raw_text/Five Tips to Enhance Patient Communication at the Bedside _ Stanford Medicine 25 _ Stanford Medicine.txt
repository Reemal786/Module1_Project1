“Making a positive connection is not always easy, especially when our days are rushed and we are stressed and
distracted, but each encounter should set the stage for respectful communication, ” explain Shannon J. Voogt,
MD, Kelli Pratt, DO, and Amber Rollet, MD, in an article for the American Academy of Family Physicians.
They recommend beginning patient interactions by warmly introducing yourself and your role while maintaining
eye contact. It can be helpful to ask how the patient would like to be addressed and acknowledge anyone
accompanying them. Taking an additional moment to make a non-medical connection can also be impactful –
try commenting on or asking a question about a neutral topic, such as the weather or sports.
