What is the respiration rate?
The respiration rate is the number of breaths a person takes per minute. The rate is usually measured when a person is
at rest and simply involves counting the number of breaths for one minute by counting how many times the chest rises.
Respiration rates may increase with fever , illness, and other medical conditions. When checking respiration, it is
important to also note whether a person has any di�culty breathing.
Normal respiration rates for an adult person at rest range from 12 to 16 breaths per minute.
